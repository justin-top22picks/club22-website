How It Works

Four steps to ruling your home course

Real rounds, real courses, real rivalries. No simulation, just the game you already play.

1

Play a round

Open the app at any of 12,400+ courses. We track your round automatically.

2

Claim the course

Finish your round and the course becomes your territory on the map.

3

Defend your turf

When others play your course, they challenge your claim. Play again to hold it.

4

Climb the board

Earn badges, build streaks, and compete with friends across the whole country.

Three Ways to Play

One map, three games

Switch modes any time. Each one tells a different story of where you've played.

Territory

Own the course

See who owns each course on the map. Play a round to claim unclaimed courses or challenge someone else's claim.

Collector

Fill the map

Track every course you've ever played. Light up the country one round at a time and build your collection across all 50 states.

Leaders

Climb the ranks

See who's on top at each course. The more rounds you play, the higher you climb on the leaderboard.
