Delete Your Club 22 Account

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

You can permanently delete your Club 22 account and associated data at any time.

In-App (Preferred)

  1. Open the Club 22 app and sign in
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom
  3. Tap the Settings gear in the top right
  4. Scroll to Danger Zone
  5. Tap Delete Account
  6. Confirm twice to complete deletion

Your account is deleted immediately. No further action needed.

By Email (Alternative)

If you can't access the app or are no longer signed in, email us at privacy@club22.app with:

  • The email address associated with your Club 22 account
  • A request to delete your account

We'll process the request within 7 business days and email you confirmation.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile, username, photo, and territory color
  • All your claimed territories (returned to "unclaimed" so other players can claim them)
  • Your play history, rounds, and badges earned
  • Your friends list and any pending friend requests
  • Photos you've uploaded
  • All notification tokens and preferences
  • Your Firebase Auth account (sign-in credentials)

What's Retained

  • Purchase records (for tax, audit, and chargeback purposes — anonymized after deletion)
  • Aggregate / anonymized usage data already de-linked from your account
